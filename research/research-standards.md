# Research Standards

Last Updated: 2026-09-09

This is a documentation protocol, not a trading system. The [original brief](phase-1-brief.md) is authoritative for the full requested scope.

1. Select one authorized category; inspect repository guidance, the index, related files and alternate names before adding a document.
2. Prefer original research and official documentation. Open the relevant page, and record exactly what was reviewed. Abstract-only access must not be described as full-paper verification.
3. Separate FACT, EMPIRICAL FINDING, THEORETICAL EXPLANATION, TRADER INTERPRETATION, ANECDOTAL CLAIM, HYPOTHESIS and PARAMETER TO TEST. Derived mathematics must state assumptions. Synthetic examples are not performance data.
4. Attach important claims to direct source links. Register organization, title, author when available, URL, publication/update dates and access date. Say not stated or not verified instead of inferring dates from crawler timestamps.
5. Seek counterevidence and alternative explanations. Record disagreements, both positions, support, shared assumptions, differences, possible causes and distinguishing tests. Use NEEDS_REVIEW while unresolved.
6. Evidence grades: STRONG = direct authoritative mechanism or robust relevant evidence; MODERATE = credible but limited, indirect or incompletely inspected evidence; WEAK = materially limited methodology; ANECDOTAL = uncontrolled testimony; UNKNOWN = no adequate evidence located. Grade mechanics and predictive efficacy separately when necessary. Absence of evidence is not evidence of no effect.
7. Knowledge statuses: RESEARCHED, NEEDS_REVIEW, INCOMPLETE. Every actual strategy has Status: RESEARCHED; Backtest Status: NOT TESTED; Validation Status: NOT VALIDATED. Never promote strategies during Phase 1.
8. Keep one canonical strategy specification under strategies/researched/. Knowledge family notes link to it. Group equivalent aliases before creating files. Never populate candidates, validated or rejected classifications in this phase.
9. Record costs, units, data timing, subjectivity, failure modes and future test parameters. Never invent optimal settings or performance statistics. External reported performance remains attributed and unreplicated by this project.
10. Update knowledge/INDEX.md and research/research-log.md each batch. Verify links, front matter, source IDs, coverage, duplication and phase boundaries. Report completion and stop.

Templates are scaffolds, not researched knowledge. Replace every placeholder before publishing a substantive document. Do not create placeholder knowledge pages merely to fill directories. Future-phase directories contain only `.gitkeep` markers until their phase is authorized.

No live market integration, trade selection, execution, backtesting or strategy formalization occurs under this protocol. Test proposals identify future questions without implementing or running them.
